MAUV Privacy Policy

Service: MAUV

Operator: 제이에이치파트너스 (English display name: JH Partners), a sole proprietorship

Privacy contact: jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com

Effective date: This Privacy Policy is effective as of November 2, 2026.

MAUV is a camera and photo editing app. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it. It is provided in Korean and English, and both versions say the same thing. MAUV 1.0 is first released for Android through Google Play; an iOS version has not been released yet and is planned for the future. What this policy says about iOS applies once an iOS version is released.

1. Summary

  • MAUV has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your photos are edited on your device and saved to your own photo library.
  • MAUV's own code does not send your photos, or information about you, to the operator or to anyone else. The operator does not collect your personal information.
  • This version of MAUV contains no analytics, advertising or payment software.
  • MAUV is intended for people aged 16 and over (section 12).

2. What MAUV accesses, and why

Camera. Used to show the live preview and to take photos, only while the camera screen is open and the app is in front. MAUV takes still photos only. It does not record video or audio and does not ask for the microphone.

A photo you choose. When you tap the photo button, your device's own photo picker opens. MAUV receives only the photo you pick. It does not ask for access to the rest of your library. On Android, the picker component places a temporary copy of the photo you pick in MAUV's private cache so the app can open it (see section 4).

Saving a photo. When you save, MAUV adds a new photo to your library. It does not change or delete photos that are already there. On iOS it asks for permission to add photos. On Android 10 and earlier it asks for permission to write to storage; on newer versions no permission is needed.

Device language. Read so the app can be shown in English or Korean.

Vibration. Used for a short tap when you take a photo or change film.

MAUV does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth, or permission to track you across other apps and websites.

4. How your photos are handled

  • On your device. Films and adjustments are applied on your device. MAUV does not upload photos.
  • Temporary files. A photo you take is written to a temporary file inside the app while you review or edit it. It is deleted shortly after you leave those screens. If you do not save it, it is gone.
  • Saved photos. A saved photo is a new file in your device's photo library, where you control it. MAUV also keeps one copy of the most recent saved photo in its cache so it can show it to you; it is replaced by the next save and your device may clear it at any time. While a new copy is being made it briefly exists next to the previous one, and in the rare case that two saves overlap and finish out of order, two copies can remain until the next save. When the app starts, it also removes copies left over from an earlier session.
  • Your originals. A photo you open from your library is only read. MAUV never changes or deletes it.
  • Copy of a photo you pick. On Android, the photo picker makes a private copy of the photo you pick inside MAUV's own storage so the app can open it. MAUV removes that copy when you leave the screen that uses it, and clears any copy left over from an earlier session the next time the app starts. Your original photo is never deleted, and neither is a photo you saved. A copy can remain if the app is closed abnormally or if removal fails, until the next start or until your device clears the cache or you uninstall the app. Whether and when the device clears the cache depends on the system.
  • Details inside saved photos. Photos saved by MAUV are newly encoded files. They are written without the embedded details (EXIF) that a camera photo normally carries, such as location, device model and time taken.
  • Cloud photo services. If your device backs up or syncs your library, for example with iCloud Photos or Google Photos, that service handles your saved photos under its own policy. MAUV is not involved.

5. What MAUV stores about you

Nothing beyond the files described above. MAUV has no user profile, keeps no history of your edits, and does not remember your settings between sessions.

Backups. MAUV turns off Android's automatic app backup, so MAUV's own files are not copied to a Google account backup. This does not affect photos you saved to your photo library; those follow your device's and your photo service's own settings.

Diagnostic messages. When something goes wrong (for example a save fails), MAUV writes a short message to your device's system log. MAUV does not collect, upload or send these messages.

6. Network and third-party libraries

MAUV's own code makes no network requests. MAUV is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native, camera and graphics libraries, among others) that run inside the app on your device. In what we have checked, none of them was found to send your information off the device, but we do not guarantee that every case has been checked. The network permission (INTERNET) and the network-state permission (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) that appear in the Android app's list are declared by the app framework and its libraries, and are not by themselves a sign that information is sent.

The privacy policy link on the About screen opens this policy in your device's web browser. The browser, not MAUV, makes that connection, and MAUV sends nothing when you tap it.

This privacy policy web page is provided through GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP address of visitors to its websites, including this page, for security purposes, and that information may be processed in your region, in the United States and in other countries where GitHub operates. That happens when you visit this web page; it is not something the MAUV app does. The MAUV app sends nothing when you tap the policy link, and the connection is made by your device's web browser. For how GitHub handles information, please read GitHub's privacy statement (the GitHub General Privacy Statement).

The list of open-source libraries and their licenses is in the app: the info button on the camera screen, then Open source licenses.

7. Analytics, advertising, payments and sharing with others

This version of MAUV does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or payment software, and it does not share your information with other companies. We do not sell personal information.

MAUV 1.0 for Android has no Share button. In the iOS version, the Share button hands the saved photo to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as optional purchases or advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

MAUV 1.0 is distributed through Google Play. When an iOS version is released, it will also be distributed through the Apple App Store. Those stores and your device's operating system may collect information such as downloads, crash reports and usage statistics under their own policies, depending on your device settings. They may show the operator totals that do not identify you.

8. Keeping and deleting

MAUV keeps nothing about you on the operator's servers, so there is nothing for the operator to delete. To remove what is on your device, delete the photos you saved from your photo library, and uninstall the app to remove its temporary and cached files. Temporary files and copies that MAUV made are removed by the app deleting those files (section 4).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information. Because MAUV is designed not to send personal information to the operator, the operator generally holds nothing to act on. To ask about your rights, or if you believe the operator holds information about you, write to jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com, say which request you are making, and the operator will answer as the law requires. Photos you saved to your photo library can be deleted by you at any time. MAUV's temporary files and cached copies are managed automatically by the app (section 4), and they are also removed when your device clears the cache or you uninstall the app.

10. Security

MAUV does not send your photos to a server, so server security does not apply. The app's temporary files and cache are in the app's private storage that Android keeps separate for each app. A saved photo is in your photo library and is protected by your device lock and library settings.
